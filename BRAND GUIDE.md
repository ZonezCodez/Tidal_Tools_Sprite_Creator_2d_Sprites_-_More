# Sprite Creator Brand Guide

## Official application mark

The official mark is the neon pixel brush emblem on a dark rounded-square field. Use `assets/sprite-creator.png` for UI/marketing and `assets/sprite-creator.ico` for Windows application identity.

## Core colors
- Onyx background: `#050608`
- Primary surface: `#0C0E12`
- Electric lime: `#C8FF3E`
- Ultraviolet: `#B69AFF`
- Hot coral: `#FF6687`
- Solar amber: `#FFC65D`
- Onyx canvas surround: `#25282E`
- Onyx gray transparency cells: `#747B84` / `#636A73`

## Usage
- Preserve the square aspect ratio. Do not stretch or recolor the mark.
- Use the ICO for Windows shortcuts, taskbar, installer and executable identity.
- Keep sufficient clear space around the mark.
- Do not add text inside the app icon; use the separate Sprite Creator wordmark in UI layouts.

## Canvas color treatment
- In the Onyx artboard mode, the artboard remains gray; the surrounding workspace is neutral graphite (`#25282E`).
- In the Prism artboard mode, the artboard remains a light transparency checker; the surrounding workspace is neutral gray (`#C5C9CF`).
- Transparency checkers are part of the artboard preview and are never baked into PNG exports.
