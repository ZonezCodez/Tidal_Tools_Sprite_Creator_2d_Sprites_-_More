SPRITE CREATOR — SOCIAL LINKS

When official social profiles are ready, edit src/brand-config.js.
Add real HTTPS accounts to the socials array. Example:
  { name: "YouTube", handle: "@yourchannel", url: "https://www.youtube.com/@yourchannel" },
  { name: "Discord", handle: "Join the community", url: "https://discord.gg/yourinvite" }

Do not distribute placeholder or fabricated handles. Empty accounts show a
small "Social links coming soon" message on the goodbye screen.
